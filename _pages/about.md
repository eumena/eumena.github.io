I am a PhD candidate in Economics at Princeton University, interested in macroeconomics, firm dynamics  and international trade.  

My research focuses on how decisions by individual firms affect aggregate economic outcomes. I study how firms' heterogeneity in technology and market power influences an economy's innovation, human capital accumulation and supply chain resilience, and I quantify the implications for welfare.
